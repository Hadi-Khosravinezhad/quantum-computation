### 🎯 Motivation & Scope
With the rapid emergence of quantum hardware and quantum optics, bridging classical engineering concepts with quantum paradigms is crucial. This primer was authored as an autonomous academic project to unpack quantum computation through rigorous matrix mechanics and circuit representations, serving as a pedagogical resource for Persian-speaking scholars.

---

### 📑 Summary of Covered Chapters 

#### Chapter 1: Introduction to Quantum Computation
- Foundations of computation, Turing machines, and information units (Bits vs. Qubits).
- Physical manifestations of qubits: Superposition, state space, and measurement collapse.
- Foundational experiments: Double-slit interference and the Stern–Gerlach effect.

#### Chapter 2: Quantum Mechanics & Linear Algebra Formalism
- Vector spaces, Dirac notation (Bra-Ket), inner/outer products, and projector operators.
- Spectral theory, Hermitian operators (observables), and Unitary dynamics.
- The 5 Postulates of Quantum Mechanics and essential single-qubit logic gates ($X, Z, H, S, T$).
- Quantum entanglement, Bell states, and the Density Operator formalism ($\rho$).
- Step-by-step mathematical derivation of the **Quantum Teleportation Protocol**.

#### Chapter 3: Quantum Computing & Search Algorithms
- Principles of Quantum Parallelism, oracles, and the phase kickback mechanism.
- Deterministic speedup: **Deutsch** and **Deutsch–Jozsa** algorithms.
- **Grover’s Search Algorithm:** Amplitude amplification, 2D geometric rotation analysis, optimal iteration bounds ($m \approx \frac{\pi}{4}\sqrt{N}$), and over-rotation dynamics.

#### Chapter 4: Quantum Fourier Transform & Advanced Algorithms
- **Quantum Fourier Transform (QFT):** Mathematical product representation and circuit synthesis.
- **Quantum Phase Estimation (QPE):** Circuit design, controlled unitary operations, and accuracy bounds.
- **Quantum Counting:** Combining Grover's operator with QPE to estimate target solution counts.
